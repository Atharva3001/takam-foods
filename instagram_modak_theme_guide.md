# Takam Ganapati Modak Instagram Theme Guide

Every themed image is composed at **9:16**, so it can be used directly as a Reel cover or Story. For a feed post, crop the central artwork to **4:5**; keep the character and its small flavour props inside the central area. The upper quarter is intentionally open for your Marathi headline, while the lower strip works well for a short product label or order prompt.

## Posting System

Keep the **Takam logo** as a small badge at the bottom left and reserve the bottom right for a simple call to action such as **“Order on WhatsApp”**. Use the same chunky black-outlined Devanagari type and do not add more than one headline plus one small supporting line. Each Reel can follow the same rhythm: the Modak introduces itself, a close-up or making shot shows the texture, then the frame returns to the themed character as the order prompt appears.

## Suggested On-Screen Sequence

| Time | On-screen moment |
| --- | --- |
| 0–2 seconds | Mascot poster with the flavour's opening line |
| 2–7 seconds | Real ingredients or preparation shot |
| 7–12 seconds | Finished Modak close-up and one taste or texture statement |
| 12–15 seconds | Mascot returns with a short ordering prompt |
