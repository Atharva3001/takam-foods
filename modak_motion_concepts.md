# Seven-Flavour Modak Motion Concepts

| Modak | Transition motion | Best Reel moment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| डिंक मोदक | **Power Pop** — a compact squat, fast vertical jump, and proud flex on landing. | Introduce a “strong taste” or traditional ingredient fact. |
| नाचणी मोदक | **Workout Shuffle** — two quick sporty side steps followed by a happy bicep pose. | Move from ingredient prep to a healthy-flavour reveal. |
| ड्रायफ्रुट मोदक | **VIP Bow** — a smooth entrance, tiny royal bow, then a confident wave out. | Reveal premium gifting or a festive product box. |
| बीट मोदक | **Pink Twirl** — a playful half spin, pose, and peace-sign exit. | Cut into a bright colour reveal or trend-led talking point. |
| पोष्टीक मोदक | **Hero Landing** — rise into view, superhero stance, and a tiny cape-like bounce. | Lead into a nourishing, feel-good story. |
| तीळकुंद मोदक | **Tiny Rocket** — rapid hop in, energetic side wobble, then a strong forward point. | Land a quick flavour fact or punchline. |
| गुलकंद मोदक | **Rose Sway** — a gentle romantic side sway, small bow, and graceful slide away. | Transition into a sweet taste description or gifting shot. |

All clips use a flat green-screen background so they can be keyed above any Takam post or Reel footage.
