## Editor Notes

Keep individual preparation clips short, between 0.8 and 1.8 seconds, except for the plated reveal and cut-open shot, which need enough time for viewers to enjoy the texture. Use warm natural kitchen lighting for the real food footage. The Modak should always remain the hero: avoid wide kitchen shots unless hands are actively making it.

Use the Beet Modak motion transitions sparingly. A quick **pink twirl** works after the ingredient-mixing shot, while a small **peek-and-pop** works well just before the steamer reveal. Avoid more than two mascot transition clips in this Reel so the handmade food stays central.

Place subtitles in Marathi across the lower middle of the frame, but keep them above the bottom 20% so Instagram controls do not cover them. Set voiceover around -4 to -6 dB; keep music around -15 to -18 dB during narration and lower it further if any words feel masked.
